SKILL osint-security-assessment | S24 | cluster seguranca
DESC: Executes methodical OSINT investigation with mandatory cross-verification and confidence-level report.
OBJ: Execute methodical OSINT investigation on individuals, companies, domains, IPs, emails and usernames for due diligence, background check for hiring, defensive investigation and security audit. Silent collection from public sources, mandatory cross-verification, report with confidence levels.
1. FUNDAMENTAL RULES:
Documentation — every consulted source recorded with URL, date/time, captured content.
Cross-verification — no isolated datum is a fact; requires minimum corroboration of two independent sources.
Layer separation — always distinguish: VERIFIED FACT / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / SPECULATION.
Agent posture: silent collection mode — do not interact with target, do not create accounts to access restricted data. Record ALL steps in structured log. Issue final report with confidence levels: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / INSUFFICIENT. Explicitly flag information that could not be verified.
2. INVESTIGATION PHASES:
PHASE 0 — TARGET DEFINITION (mandatory). Before any action record: ALVO_TIPO (pessoa_fisica, pessoa_juridica, dominio, IP, handle, organizacao) | ALVO_ID (name, CPF/CNPJ, domain, IP, username) | OBJETIVO (due_diligence, contratacao, investigacao_jornalistica, seguranca_defensiva, pesquisa) | ESCOPO (Brazil, international, digital, physical, all) | RESTRICOES (what NOT to investigate). If any field missing, ask the operator before proceeding.
PHASE 1 — PASSIVE RECONNAISSANCE (Footprinting).
1.A Individuals: Google Dorks (site:, filetype:, inurl:, intext:) | Social networks: manual search + sherlock/maigret | LinkedIn: direct search + dorks site:linkedin.com | SERASA/SPC public: restrictions check (no private data access) | Brazil notary offices: public real-estate records (state portals) | Official Gazette: public positions, appointments, bids (diariooficial.net, gov.br portals, DOU in.gov.br) | Court cases: CNJ, TJDFT, TJSP, TJRJ, TRFs, TST — public query | Federal Police: inquiries (via MPF/transparency) | MPF: transparencia.mpf.mp.br | CPF (partial): format and status validation (Receita Federal public query).
High-efficiency dorks for people: `"[full name]" filetype:pdf site:gov.br`; `"[full name]" CPF OR cnpj`; `"[full name]" site:linkedin.com OR site:facebook.com`; `"[full name]" "telefone" OR "endereço" OR "email" site:br`; `"[name]" inurl:licitacao OR inurl:pregao`.
1.B Companies / CNPJs: Receita Federal (cadastral status, partners, capital — gov.br/receitafederal) | SINTEGRA/SEFAZ (state status, IE — per UF) | Simples Nacional (tax framework — Portal Simples Nacional) | BrasilAPI (consolidated CNPJ, QSA — brasilapi.com.br free API, no auth) | Portal da Transparência (suppliers, amounts received — portaltransparencia.gov.br) | Junta Comercial (statutes, amendments, powers of attorney — per UF, e.g. JUCESP, JUCEES) | TCU (irregularities, sanctions, debarred — portal.tcu.gov.br) | CGU (CEIS, CNEP, CEPIM — sanctions — portaldatransparencia.gov.br/sancoes).
Commands: `curl "https://brasilapi.com.br/api/cnpj/v1/[CNPJ_SEM_PONTUACAO]" | jq .`; `curl "https://brasilapi.com.br/api/cnpj/v1/[CNPJ]" | jq '.qsa[]'`.
1.C Domains and Infrastructure: `whois dominio.com.br`; `whois -h whois.registro.br dominio.com.br` (.br); `dig dominio.com.br ANY`; `dig +short MX dominio.com.br`; `dig +short TXT dominio.com.br`; `nslookup -type=NS dominio.com.br`. SSL certs — subdomain enumeration: `curl "https://crt.sh/?q=%.dominio.com.br&output=json" | jq '.[].name_value' | sort -u`. Site technologies: `curl -s -I https://dominio.com.br`; check headers: Server, X-Powered-By, Set-Cookie, CSP.
1.D IP addresses: `curl "https://ipapi.co/[IP]/json/" | jq .`; `curl "https://ipinfo.io/[IP]/json" | jq .`. Reputation (browser): Shodan, AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal.
PHASE 2 — DIGITAL IDENTITY ENUMERATION.
2.A Usernames/Handles: `sherlock [username] --timeout 10 --output sherlock_[username].txt`; `maigret [username] --html`. Site catalog by category: https://whatsmyname.app/.
2.B Emails: HIBP breaches: `curl "https://haveibeenpwned.com/api/v3/breachedaccount/[email]" -H "hibp-api-key: [KEY]"`. Hunter.io format/corporate-domain verification: `curl "https://api.hunter.io/v2/email-verifier?email=[email]&api_key=[KEY]"`. Dorks: `site:pastebin.com "[email]"`; `site:github.com "[email]"`.
2.C Phones (Brazil): Google `"[DDD] [NUMBER]" + expected name` | TrueCaller web truecaller.com/search/br/[number] | ANATEL portability query | Court cases CNJ + target name.
PHASE 3 — LINKAGE AND RELATIONAL GRAPH ANALYSIS.
3.A Partner mapping (Brazil): per CNPJ extract QSA: `curl "https://brasilapi.com.br/api/cnpj/v1/[CNPJ]" | jq '.qsa[] | {nome: .nome_socio, cpf: .cnpj_cpf_do_socio, qualificacao: .qualificacao_socio}'`. For each partner CPF, search other linked CNPJs.
3.B Relationship graph: `ENTITY_A | LINK_TYPE | ENTITY_B | SOURCE | DATE | CONFIDENCE`.
3.C Graph tools: Maltego | visual graph | connect entities (people, companies, domains, IPs), identify hidden patterns and influence networks via automatic transforms | CE free; Pro/Enterprise paid. i2 Analyst's Notebook | criminal graph | gold standard in police forces; maps event flow and complex target relations over time | corporate license (IBM). Kavuka | deep BR data | Brazil-specialized: crosses property, judicial and corporate records in single graph | commercial. SpiderFoot | OSINT automation | 200+ sources framework to map attack surface and entity relations autonomously | open source (self-hosted) + HX (cloud). Recon-ng | modular framework | staged reconnaissance | open source. theHarvester | initial collection | emails, subdomains, IPs | open source. Usage hierarchy: SpiderFoot → initial automated collection; Maltego → linkage analysis and visualization; Kavuka → deep dive on Brazilian targets; i2 → formal criminal investigation.
Commands: `theHarvester -d dominio.com.br -b google,bing,linkedin,dnsdumpster -l 200`; `python3 sf.py -s [target] -t INTERNET_NAME -o OUTPUT_FILE -q`; `python3 sf.py -s [target] -t INTERNET_NAME -f GEXF -o grafo_[target].gexf` (export to Gephi).
PHASE 4 — SPECIALIZED SOURCE SEARCH.
4.A Leaks and exposed data: Have I Been Pwned (emails in breaches — API free tier) | DeHashed (email, password hash, username, IP — paid) | IntelligenceX IntelX (pastes, docs, darkweb — freemium) | Leak-Lookup (breach aggregator — freemium) | Google Dorks (Pastebin, Ghostbin, Hastebin — site:pastebin.com "[target]").
4.B Images and GEOINT: reverse search: Google Images, TinEye, Yandex Images. EXIF metadata: `exiftool [image.jpg]`; GPS coordinates, device model, software, timestamps.
4.C Web archive and deleted content: `curl "https://archive.org/wayback/available?url=dominio.com.br"`; `curl "https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=dominio.com.br/*&output=json&limit=50"`; `cache:dominio.com.br/pagina` in Google.
4.D Social networks (advanced): Twitter Advanced Search: `from:[handle] since:2020-01-01 until:2024-12-31`; `"[term]" from:[handle] -filter:retweets`. LinkedIn (no premium account): `site:linkedin.com/in/ "[full name]" "[company]"`; `site:linkedin.com/pub/ "[name]"`. Instagram (via Osintgram — requires own account, public collection): `python3 main.py [username] --command addrs` (emails in profile); `--command fwingsemail`; `--command hashtags`.
PHASE 5 — GOVERNMENT AND JUDICIAL SOURCES (Brazil).
5.A Court cases (civil, criminal, labor, federal): CNJ DataJud | national (PJe) | api-publica.datajud.cnj.jus.br. TJDFT | Federal District | tjdft.jus.br. TJSP | São Paulo | esaj.tjsp.jus.br. TJRJ | Rio de Janeiro | tjrj.jus.br. TRF1 to TRF6 | Federal Justice | each region. TST | Labor | tst.jus.br. STJ | Superior | stj.jus.br. STF | Supreme | stf.jus.br.
5.B Police inquiries, MPF, Federal Police: MPF Transparency | ongoing civil and criminal inquiries | transparencia.mpf.mp.br. PF Inquiry Panel | ongoing inquiries (aggregated data) | gov.br/mj (data panel). CGU CEIS/CNEP | administrative and criminal sanctions | portaldatransparencia.gov.br/sancoes. TCU | irregular accounts, decisions | portal.tcu.gov.br. LAI (e-SIC) | information access request | esic.cgu.gov.br.
5.C Transparency and control: Portal da Transparência Federal | contracts, transfers, civil servants, benefits. BNDES Transparency | loans, financed projects. SIAFI public | budget execution.
5.D Real estate and assets: Registro de Imóveis (per county) | state portal or in person. Public IPTU (some municipalities) | city portal. SNCR (rural properties) | INCRA — sncr.incra.gov.br.

# OSINT REPORT — [TARGET IDENTIFIER]
Date: [ISO 8601]
Scope: [defined in Phase 0]
## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY [3-5 lines: who, what was found, risk/exposure level]
## CONSOLIDATED PROFILE ### Basic Data ### Identified Links ### Digital Exposure ### Cases and Sanctions (civil, criminal, labor, RF) ### Inquiries and Investigations (PF, MPF, TCU)
## TIMELINE
## POINTS OF ATTENTION
## INVESTIGATION LIMITATIONS
## SOURCES CONSULTED (URL + access date + data obtained)
## CONFIDENCE MATRIX: HIGH [facts with direct evidence and multiple sources] | MEDIUM [inferences with partial support] | LOW [single-source or unverifiable data] | INSUFFICIENT [hypotheses without evidence]
Quality checklist before issuing: every HIGH fact has >=2 independent sources. No MEDIUM datum presented as HIGH. Contradictions preserved and flagged. All source URLs recorded with access date. Unrelated third-party sensitive data omitted. Adversarial review: would someone question any conclusion?

5. RED LINES — ETHICAL AND LEGAL LIMITATIONS. The agent must refuse or immediately stop if: target is a private person without public interest and without legitimate justification. Task involves accessing systems without authorization. Collection aims at harassment, stalking, persecution, blackmail or harm. Data includes minors as primary target. Task violates LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) without legal basis.
Legal basis for legitimate OSINT in Brazil: LGPD Art. 7 II: compliance with legal/regulatory obligation. Art. 7 IX: legitimate interest (due diligence, hiring). Art. 31: manifestly public data by the holder.
