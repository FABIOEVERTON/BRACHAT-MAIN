# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

## Overview
Voluntary framework published on Jan 26, 2023 by NIST. Focused on managing AI risks for individuals, organizations, and society.

## The 4 Core Functions

### 1. GOVERN (Gov — Cross-cutting)
Culture, processes, and governance systems:
- **Gov 1**: Policy, organizational, and stakeholder context
- **Gov 2**: Risk management processes, transparency, and accountability
- **Gov 3**: Third-party AI system risk management
- **Gov 4**: Workforce diversity, multidisciplinary teams
- **Gov 5**: AI risk management is integrated into broader enterprise risk management

### 2. MAP (Map)
Context to identify risks:
- **Map 1**: Context and intended purpose of AI system
- **Map 2**: Relevant laws, regulations, and norms
- **Map 3**: Teammates and impacts on defined uses
- **Map 4**: Mapping of AI system components and lifecycle
- **Map 5**: Mapping of risks and potential impacts

### 3. MEASURE (Measure)
Analysis, assessment, benchmark, and monitoring:
- **Meas 1**: Metrics for AI trustworthiness (valid, reliable, safe, secure, responsible, transparent, explainable, fair, accountable)
- **Meas 2**: AI system is evaluated for risk
- **Meas 3**: AI baseline is monitored over time
- **Meas 4**: Feedback mechanisms for risk identification
- **Meas 5**: Testing protocols and assurance

### 4. MANAGE (Manage)
Prioritization and action on identified risks:
- **Man 1**: Risk response strategies (accept, mitigate, transfer, avoid)
- **Man 2**: Risk treatment plans
- **Man 3**: Ongoing monitoring and improvement
- **Man 4**: Incident response and recovery
- **Man 5**: Documentation and communication of risk

## Trustworthy AI Characteristics
- **Valid and Reliable** (Valid and Reliable)
- **Safe** (Safe)
- **Secure and Resilient** (Secure and Resilient)
- **Accountable and Transparent** (Accountable and Transparent)
- **Explainable and Interpretable** (Explainable and Interpretable)
- **Privacy-Enhanced** (Privacy-Enhanced)
- **Fair with Harmful Bias Managed** (Fair with Harmful Bias Managed)

## Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1 — Jul 2024)
Specific risks: hallucination, amplified bias, deepfakes, copyright infringement, privacy, malicious use

## Profile for Critical Infrastructure (Concept — Apr 2026)
Guide for critical infrastructure operators when using AI capabilities
