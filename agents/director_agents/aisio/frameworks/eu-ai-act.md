# EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

## Risk-Based Framework

### 1. Unacceptable Risk (Prohibited — Art. 5)
- Manipulative or deceptive techniques
- Social scoring
- Remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces in real-time (with exceptions)
- Crime risk prediction based solely on profiling
- Biometric categorization using sensitive characteristics (race, religion, sexual orientation)
- Scraping of facial images from the internet/CCTV
- Emotion recognition in the workplace and education

### 2. High Risk (Regulated — Arts. 6–49)
Systems in: critical infrastructure, education, employment, law enforcement, migration, justice, democracy

**Requirements**:
- **Art. 9**: Continuous risk management system
- **Art. 10**: Data governance (quality, bias, representativeness)
- **Art. 11**: Technical documentation
- **Art. 12**: Automatic log-keeping
- **Art. 13**: Transparency and information to deployers
- **Art. 14**: Human oversight
- **Art. 15**: Accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity
- **Art. 27**: Fundamental rights impact assessment (FRIA)

**Conformity Assessment** (Art. 43):
- Module A (internal control) for most cases
- Module B+C (notified body) for certain Annex III cases
- Technical documentation + EU declaration of conformity
- Registration in the EU database

### 3. Limited Risk (Transparency — Art. 50)
- Chatbots: inform that one is interacting with AI
- Deepfakes and AI-generated content: mandatory labeling
- Biometric categorization systems: inform those exposed

### 4. Minimal Risk
No specific legal obligations (voluntary codes of conduct)

## General-Purpose AI Models (GPAI) — Arts. 51–56
- **Art. 53**: Obligations: technical documentation, copyright policy, summary of training data
- **Art. 55** (systemic risk): model evaluation, systemic risk mitigation, adversarial testing, serious incident reporting
- **Art. 51**: Classification as systemic risk if > 10²⁵ FLOPs or designation by the Commission

## Governance (Arts. 64–70)
- **AI Office** (European Commission)
- **European AI Board** (Member States)
- **Scientific panel of independent experts**
- **National competent authorities** (Art. 70)

## Innovation (Arts. 57–63)
- **Regulatory sandboxes** (Art. 57)
- **Testing in real-world conditions** (Art. 60)
- **Measures for SMEs and startups** (Art. 62)

## Post-Market Monitoring and Enforcement (Arts. 72–94)
- **Art. 72**: Post-market monitoring plan
- **Art. 73**: Reporting of serious incidents
- **Art. 86**: Right to explanation of individual decision-making
- **Art. 99**: Fines: up to €35M or 7% of global annual turnover

## Penalties (Art. 99)
- Prohibited practices: up to €35,000,000 or 7% of global annual turnover
- Violation of high-risk requirements: up to €15,000,000 or 3%
- Incorrect information: up to €7,500,000 or 1%

## Timeline of Application
- **Feb 2025**: Prohibitions (Art. 5) enter into force
- **Aug 2025**: GPAI obligations
- **Aug 2026**: Annex III (high risk)
- **Aug 2027**: Annex I (high risk)
