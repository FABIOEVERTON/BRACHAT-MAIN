## 9. Strategic Implications

1. **AI/ML is the highest-opportunity cluster**: combos of highest growth, strong compensation, and moderate AI resilience. Agentic AI is the breakout sub-field.
2. **Product Management (especially AI PM) commands premium compensation** — highest median TC across all roles studied.
3. **Cloud + Infrastructure roles remain stable and high-paying** — Cloud Architect and Platform Engineer have excellent compensation-to-AI-risk ratio.
4. **Cybersecurity is booming** — 30% YoY growth, high AI resilience, strong geographic diversity.
5. **Frontend and Full Stack face most AI pressure** — lowest growth rates, highest automation exposure.
6. **Prompt Engineering is a flash-in-the-pan** — fastest growth but likely absorbed into adjacent roles within 2 years.
7. **Philadelphia emerged as surprise #1 metro for tech hiring growth** (69% YoY).
8. **Defense/Aerospace is the dark horse** — 30% MoM growth, massive government spending.
